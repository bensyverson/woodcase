# Merged concepts

Fifty directions from five bare agents (`agent-1.md` … `agent-5.md`, prompt in `../concept-prompt.md`), merged by hand on 2026-09-30 into the concepts below. Two directions merge when they would build the same deck: the same governing idea, whatever the palette. **Convergence** is the number of agents (of five) that reached the idea. Members are cited as `agent-N: Direction name`.

Every direction kept the decided reveal (the deck's own activity log), so the reveal does not distinguish them.

| id | concept | convergence | members |
|---|---|---|---|
| K01 | **Terminal session.** Every slide is the next screen of one shell; prose lives in `#` comments; real commands and output; the reveal is `woodcase activity` on the deck. | 5 | 1: Session; 2: Session; 3: Terminal Session; 4: Terminal Transcript; 5: Session |
| K02 | **Self-annotated (X-ray).** Every element on every slide wears its own hairline rect and `tree` tag (name, rect, id), real values read back after layout; the reveal inverts or strips the layers. | 5 | 1: X-Ray; 2: Wireframe; 3: X-Ray; 4: Self-Annotated; 5: Debug overlay |
| K03 | **Text and pixels (diptych).** Every slide split at the middle: what the agent reads (text, `tree`) against what a human sees (pixels); rows tied to rects across the seam; the reveal removes the split. | 4 | 1: Diptych; 3: Text and Pixels; 4: Call and Response; 5: Two readers |
| K04 | **Title sequence.** The original use case made the form: each slide a film or broadcast title card (letterbox or title-safe guides, timecode); the reveal is end credits or an edit decision list from the log. | 4 | 1: Main Titles; 2: Title cards; 3: Title Card; 5: Main titles |
| K05 | **Ledger.** The activity log is the spine from page one: a ruled footer, band or margin on every slide carries the real log lines, revs and `--as` names that built it; the reveal lets the log take the page. | 4 | 1: Ledger; 2: Margin; 3: Revision Ledger; 4: Rev Ledger |
| K06 | **Revisions.** The deck is one artifact edited in front of the reader: each slide the same composition one `set` or one layer of fidelity later, the command printed; the reveal is every revision as a contact sheet. | 4 | 1: Revisions; 2: Flipbook; 3: One Rectangle; 4: Fidelity Ramp |
| K07 | **Woodcase, literally.** The name as the form: a printer's type case or a woodcut relief print, two inks on cream, slab wood type; the reveal drops the craft for a plain typewritten log. | 4 | 1: Type Case; 2: Woodcut; 4: Wood Type Case; 5: Woodcut |
| K08 | **Swiss poster.** International Typographic Style: a strict grid, one oversized statement or numeral per slide, one signal red; the reveal overruns the grid with the log. | 3 | 1: Swiss Index; 3: Swiss Poster; 4: Swiss Grid |
| K09 | **One canvas.** Nine slides are nine crops of one continuous artboard (a design tool's canvas, or one line running page to page); the reveal zooms out to the whole canvas with the crops outlined. | 3 | 1: One Canvas; 3: One Canvas; 5: One canvas |
| K10 | **Statement.** One sentence per slide at display size on paper, one accent word, one small piece of evidence; the reveal inverts to ink and the log fills it. | 2 | 1: One Line; 5: Statement |
| K11 | **Glow keynote.** The launch-event register: dark field, radial bloom, glassy cards, one claim per slide; the reveal turns the glow off. | 2 | 3: Glow Keynote; 4: Glow Keynote |
| K12 | **Sight earned.** The deck is set as an agent reads it, text only, and pixels arrive only where `shot` would be called; the ground brightens as the deck gains sight. | 2 | 2: Resolution; 4: Agent's Eye |
| K13 | **Verbs.** Each slide is one CLI verb set enormous and cropped by the page, over a flat band of ink color, with one caption and one artifact; the gap is a `?` at the same scale. | 1 | 2: Verbs |
| K14 | **Table.** The deck's grid is `tree`'s columns (type, name, rect, clip, id, content); the headline is one giant row among small ones; a running `rev` head. | 1 | 2: Table |
| K15 | **Engineering drawing.** Each slide a drawing sheet: title block with real rev and `DRAWN BY` the agent, dimension lines, detail balloons. | 1 | 2: Engineering drawing |
| K16 | **Diff.** Each slide is the diff from the previous one, in red and green hunks; the thrown script is a rolled-back hunk; the reveal is the commit list. | 1 | 3: Diff |
| K17 | **Loop spine.** One ring (Command, View, Iterate) in the same place on every slide, lit where the slide is; the reveal traces the ring once per log entry into a dense spiral. | 1 | 4: Loop Spine |
| K18 | **Exploded pipeline.** The engine as an isometric stack of planes (parse, resolve, expand, layout, render), built up a stage per slide. | 1 | 5: Exploded pipeline |
| K19 | **Press proof.** Each page is a printer's proof of itself: crop marks, registration targets, a color bar built from the document's variables, a real slug line (file, rev, id). | 1 | 5: Press proof |
| K20 | **Onion skin.** Every slide shows its own history: earlier revisions ghosted behind the current one at stepped opacities, taken from the log. | 1 | 5: Onion skin |

K12 merges two directions that share the rule "pixels only where `shot` would be called"; 2: Resolution adds the brightening ground, 4: Agent's Eye the single-typeface character grid. K06 merges four decks that each iterate one artifact across the slides; 3: One Rectangle is the most reduced of them.
